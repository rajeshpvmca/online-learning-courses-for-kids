OUR ORIGIN STORY

From One Kitchen Table to 45,000+ Young Creators

Stackly Kids Academy began in 2019 when our founder, Dr. Eleanor Vance, realized that children were spending 4 to 6 hours every day on digital screens consuming entertainment, yet had virtually zero opportunities to learn how those technologies actually work.

We asked a simple question: What if screen time wasn't something parents had to battle against, but a safe playground where children discovered the thrill of building their own video games, robotics rovers, and digital symphonies? Today, our certified educators guide students across 35 countries with enthusiasm, warmth, and boundless curiosity.

EDUCATIONAL ARCHITECTURE

The "Tinker, Build, Reflect" Learning Loop

Our curriculum blends Seymour Papert's Constructionism theory with modern gamification. Rather than asking kids to passively memorize abstract concepts, we place them immediately into creator mode:

Stage 1: Guided Discovery (Tinker)

Kids dissect working game mechanics or science puzzles to understand how components interact.

Stage 2: Hands-On Creation (Build)

Students build their own unique twist on the project, writing real code or wiring physical sensors.

Stage 3: Peer Showcase (Reflect)

Children present their project to their mentor and family, articulating their thought process with pride.
